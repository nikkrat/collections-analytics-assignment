Leadership conclusion & recommended action

The reported “11% MoM improvement” is real arithmetic for Feb→Mar, but it is not evidence of a sustained business improvement.

Recommended action: correct reporting methodology immediately by showing any MoM headline alongside the trailing 6-month range. For the ₹10 Cr investment question, run a properly randomized 90-day pilot (₹1–1.5 Cr) for AI voice automation versus the current mix before committing the full amount. Measure recovery-per-₹-spent with real cost tracking.

Why not a definitive ROI? The source data contains no usable cost field, so incremental recovery, ROI and break-even cannot be estimated with useful precision without assumptions.
